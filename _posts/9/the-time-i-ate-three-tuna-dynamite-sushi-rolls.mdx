One time<br />
I ate three tuna dynamite sushi rolls at my older brother’s 14th birthday party then threw up on my mom’s bedroom carpet.<br />
<br />
When Jesus suffered in the Garden of Gethsemane<br />
He was bound by the weight of all mankind’s sins<br />
So he could grant unto us <br />
The cleansing power of repentance.<br />
The Bible says he sweated blood<br />
Though I know not if that is true.<br />
<br />
Then cometh Ethan unto a place called his parents’ bedroom,<br />
And saith unto his parents “I feel not well”<br />
And lo, he spake unto his father saying “O my Father, if it be possible, <br />
Let this cup pass from me<br />
Nevertheless not as I will, but as thou wilt.”<br />
And Ethan did feel a great burden lifted from him<br />
That he did suffer no more.<br />
<br />
The vomit was deep red, flecked silver white:<br />
Judas’s thirty pieces scattered on the carpet.<br />
And I tasted the acid of the vinegar sponge as if it was me on the cross<br />
And a stabbing spear in my side.<br />
<br />
The Bible says Jesus was bound by all of mankind’s sins.<br />
Was he gluttonous?<br />
Did he dishonor his father and mother?<br />
Does this mean he experienced the shame of<br />
Eating three tuna dynamite sushi rolls and then throwing up on his mom’s bedroom carpet?<br />

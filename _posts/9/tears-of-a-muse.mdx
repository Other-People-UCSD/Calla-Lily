title: Tears of a muse
contributor: 'Camila De La Cerda Rodriguez'

an empty canvas stares at the muse<br />
like a fleshless soul haunting the blood in her veins<br />
splattered paint taints the sun and sheds light on her rotten hues<br />
<br />
tears of a muse that poison the lamented<br />
the agony of the dead claws at her skin<br />
her throat mirroring the caws of the ravens in their nest<br />
<br />
she bears the mourning of a million sorrows<br />
her love was forsaken when he cleaved her heart for hell’s fire<br />
a possessed being he became for the love he borrowed<br />
<br />
romantic delusions weaved her blind delight<br />
the hands of God could not lift the veil that left her mystified<br />
while the devil guarded his heart like a macabre knight<br />
<br />
tears of an artist who picks up his paint brush from Hades lethe<br />
the apathy he wields stabs her in reverence for the dead<br />
a man whose heart oozes stale blood can only steal her last breath<br />
<br />
the love of a man is contempt that has been sealed<br />
his art is an ode to those who detest their lover’s hearts<br />
the wood from his canvas is the devil’s shield <br />
<br />
oh how he paints!<br />
he dips his brush and paints and paints until her eyes are vacant<br />
the muse, <i>his</i> muse, has become a specter of his own craft<br />
<br />
one after another the blots of paint mock her figure<br />
his still gaze consumes the wounds on her breast <br />
the angels suffocate her and capture her last whimper<br />
<br />
tears of the dead that cradle her lips as she looks up at the moon<br />
the resentment written on her tongue becomes her rune<br />
the corpse on his canvas can only smile as his love withers away too soon<br />

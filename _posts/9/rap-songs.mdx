<b>Act I.</b><br />


On my birthday,<br />
Red doctors logged onto interface<br />
and checked off cut <br />
of my umbilical cord. <br />

<div style="text-align: end; padding-right: 25%">

They left Umma’s tongue out<br />

after intestine dinner.<br />

<div style="text-align: end; padding-right: 25%">
Her tongue licked my belly,<br />

‘BLOOD-MUD <br />
PHENOMENON’<br />

They let me go<br />

still, <br />
and with my mother’s tongue.<br />

The tongue a witness,<br />

<b>Act IV.</b><br />

The dinner table is wet<br />
and coated slick<br />
with yellow-Red discomfort, Neosporin, <br />
and an overturned pig.<br />

<div style="text-align: end; padding-right: 25%">

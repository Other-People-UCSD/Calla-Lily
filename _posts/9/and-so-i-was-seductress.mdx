<div>
<pre>                                                                                                      ..and so,</pre>

<pre>                                                                                                                       perhaps,</pre>
<pre>                                                                                                                                 I was a seductress in my past life..</pre>

<pre>                                                         For it was before I knew the name for flesh</pre>

<pre>                                                                                                                                      that I knew  to fear it being seen.</pre>

<pre>                                           A choir of voices</pre>
<pre>                                                                          reaching its crescendo</pre>
<pre>                                                                                        once skies of lustful stars appear to me.</pre>

<pre>                                                                                                                                                                    “<i>shame</i>”, they say.</pre>

<pre>                                                                                                                                                                                                  A silent song of evil curses,</pre>

<pre>                                                                                                                                      in an ancient language my soul no longer knows,</pre>

<pre>                                                                       weaving between twinkling bliss,</pre>

<pre>        then disappearing just as fast as it had all come.</pre>

<pre>                                                                                                       “<i>shame</i>”, they sing.</pre>

<pre>Under a falling sky of words and bitter breath,</pre>

<pre>                                                            rancid smells fill my youthful lungs,</pre>

<pre>                                                                                                                                               I become so small</pre>
<pre>                                                                                                                                                               and less of who I once was.</pre>

<pre>                                                           ..so perhaps</pre>
<pre>                                                                          I was a seductress in my past life..</pre>

<pre>For it was before I could open mine,</pre>
<pre>                              I knew the itch and scratch of leering eyes.</pre>

<pre>                                                                                         How else do I explain this instinct,</pre>

<pre>                                                                                                                      the sixth sense,</pre>
<pre>                                                                                                                                     for the distant heat of another body no matter how far,</pre>
<pre>                                                                                                                                                                                                                              dark,</pre>
<pre>                                                                                                                                                                                                                              or quiet the night is..</pre>

<pre>                                                                                                                                Surely it must be an ancient me that lingers,</pre>

<pre>                                                                                                               it must be something from within that speaks,</pre>

<pre>                                                           how else can I explain this odd frequency,?</pre>

<pre>                                                                                                                                                     <i>Shame..</i></pre>
<pre>                                                                                                                                    I, a seductress, in a world with a</pre>
<pre>                                                                                                                                                                                 leering gaze.</pre>

<pre>                                                                                                                                              Surely it’s so,</pre>

<pre>                                                                                                                     it must be so,</pre>
<pre>                                                                                                                                   it could only be so</pre>

<pre>                                                                                                                                                so</pre>
<pre>                                                                                                                                                   so many eyes</pre>
<pre>                                                                                                                                                         so little was I.</pre>

<pre>                                                                                                                 Mother Magdalene,</pre>

<pre>                                                                                                          is this world rotting or am I?</pre>
</div>

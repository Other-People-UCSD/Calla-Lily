<center>This is an epic love story.</center>

You were everything.<br />
Did I tell you that?<br />
You lived under my <br />
skin and took over <br />
all sense and reason.<br />
Did I tell you that enough?<br />

<center>She was glued to his hip.</center>
<center>She began acting like him.</center>

I love you.<br />
The way you dressed<br />
The way you smelled<br />
The way you parted your hair<br />
The way you smiled<br />
The way y—<br />

<center>She, maybe, even wanted to be him. </center>
<center>Concern—for him—grew.</center>

—ou placed your toothbrush on my sink, always slanted
<p style="margin-left:40px;  margin-top: -0.25em">to the right</p>
<div style="display:flex; margin-bottom: -2em; margin-top: -2em">
<p>The way you shower with the curtain</p>
<p style="margin-left:40px">slightly open</p>
</div>
The way you always took your time shedding clothes, shoes, and bags at the door<br />
the way you scratched the back of your ear when you were hungry<br />

<center>She disappeared.</center>
<center>She became a man, rumors say.</center>

The way you avoided people’s eyes when you <s>know</s> knew no one in the room, the way<br />
you didn’t know math very well and always ask the simplest questions<br />
the way you <s>forget</s> forgot words after watching happy movies, but,<br />
could talk on and on about sad movies,<br />
the way you <br />
write poems<br />
with random breaks in lines and<br />
no punctuation<br />

<center>He was the love of her life. </center>
<center>He was her life.</center>
<center>But… some claim he didn’t know her.</center>
<center>Not truly.</center>

the way you love the clouds when they bring night in early <br />
the way you sometimes leave the door open after you leave the house the way you <br />
never listen to me when i tell you you’re wrong the way you smile at me when you’re not paying <br />
attention the way you scrunch your nose at oranges and spinach—<br />
<p style="margin-left:40px;  margin-top: -5px; margin-bottom: -5px">now i scrunch my nose too</p>
—the way people don’t see you the way i do<br />

<center>We don’t know where she went when he died.</center>
<center>How could she live without him?</center>

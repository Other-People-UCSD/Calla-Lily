I lie,<br />
Blanketed in sand,<br />
Sides painted the dull sky blue—<br />
<br />
They wouldn’t have seen me coming.<br />
<br />
Something inside - twisted wrong,<br />
Metal unlatched to caress the dirt.<br />
<br />
My life extended beyond my function—<br />
Rendered harmless—<br />
<br />
My body emblazoned, marked by my creator,<br />
A scrawled signature,<br />
Flush to my underbelly.<br />
<br />
Dust settles.<br />
The people I was born to destroy gather around me,<br />
Confused, afraid—<br />
They have not been sent any miracles<br />
In a very long time.<br />
<br />
A child curiously touches the tip of my nose.<br />
Would we have died together?<br />
Her palm is warm, girlhood still pink across her cheeks.<br />
<br />
\\I hope that I will never fulfill my purpose\\<br />
<br />
Let me return this metal body to the earth,<br />
Let these children continue living,<br />
Let my brothers also fail and fall gently,<br />
Let there be no more death.<br />
<br />
Tonight, let the night sky remain illuminated<br />
Only by stars.<br />
I dream of the parts within me, mined deep in the ground.<br />
<div style="display:flex">
<p>Even the stones will resist.</p> 						
<p style="margin-left: 300px;">Yes, even the stones.</p>
</div>

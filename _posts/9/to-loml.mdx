<center>REVISION WORKSHEET: Physical Process</center><br>
<center>Candidate Name:  Zinnia Wu</center> <br>
<b>Section 1: Key Terminology</b> <br>

Define the following terms in the context of mechanical weathering and landscape formation: </pre>
<pre>1. REGOLITH:               my drenched mascara,   </pre>                                                                 
<pre>               mantles of my skin peeled off  </pre>                                     
<pre>                   in front of you.                Flocs of me scattered…  </pre>                            
<pre>                        unconsolidated.  </pre>  

<br>

<pre>2. EXFOLIATION / EXUVIATION:  and the successive exuviation of disguise</pre>
<pre>proceeds      as we simply cannot</pre>                                             
<pre>              withstand </pre>                                                         
<pre>              the internal pressure to declare.         I disintegrate;</pre>                                                                  
<pre>                                                   you shift your eyes </pre>                      
<pre>to hide a tear.  </pre>     

<br>

<pre>3. AEOLIAN ABRASION (MUSHROOM ROCK):    I’ve heard laughs; </pre>                
<pre>            People say you look like an alien        (sometimes, </pre>                 
<pre>                                                act, too)  </pre>                 
<pre>     But the way you cautiously           raise your forearms to  </pre>                                                          
<pre>                                        cover me – undercut by the wind –  </pre>      
<pre>     awakens my incarnation   </pre>                                                                   
<pre>     of being a granite.                I feel pedestalized  </pre>                        
<pre>                                     in your hug.   </pre>                      

<br>

<pre>4. THERMAL FLUX:   With confession,         you symbolize your cavity to</pre>
<pre>                           a microwave, teeming with</pre>                               
<pre>                   lukewarm mechanics                   faked as heat   </pre>     
<pre>                      and it cooks        but never warms you.</pre>              

<br>

<pre>5. DIURNAL TEMPERATURE RANGE:        To which I console:  </pre>                   
<pre>               our late-night talking is our tropical niche, </pre>                         
<pre>                        the only shelter we will survive. </pre>                                 
<pre>                             Large diurnal temperature change </pre>                             
<pre>                                             will clot and scab our</pre>                               
<pre>                    cuts</pre>                                        
<pre>                                              loosen, nearly drop. </pre>   

<br>

<pre><b>Section 2: Synthesis</b></pre>
<pre>Explain how these processes contribute to the final state of the landscape: </pre>
<pre>     And when our tears merge & clink like wine glasses on the floor,  </pre>                                  
<pre>                 (underage, nothing but quivering mountain dew)    </pre>                             
<pre>   It will be more than weatherings that take place:   </pre>                                         
<pre>                washings, meaningless talkings,  </pre>                                         
<pre>                   silent spars, dreamings,</pre>                                           
<pre>        uncountable wakings, sleepings, murmurings – </pre>                                          
<pre>    every pore on my skin is now tears.              You breeze,</pre>                                           
<pre>                                     and crystals spring in me</pre>                                             
<pre> like ivies.</pre>                                                                                                                               
<pre>      We’re never ignorant.          All the natural phenomena exist  </pre>                                 
<pre>            to create us.         This is our geography,</pre>                                                                                                                               
<pre>                         our little universe.               </pre>
